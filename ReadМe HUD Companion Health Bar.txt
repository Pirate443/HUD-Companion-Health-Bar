--------------------------------------------------------------
			Description:
This mod adds a companion health bar interface. For each companion, it displays bars for health, magicka, and stamina, as well as a slot showing their currently equipped weapon or spell. This mod is an enhanced version of the "More Detailed Companion HealthBars " mod by Spammer21.
Mod Features
- The mod configuration menu has been completely overhauled.
- Many HUD parameters can now be customized in detail.
- Different setting configurations can be saved to separate files, allowing for quick switching between them.
- Several ready-made presets are included.
